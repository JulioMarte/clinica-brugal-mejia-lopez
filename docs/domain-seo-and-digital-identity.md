# Clínica Brugal — Domain, Digital Identity, and SEO Naming Decisions

Status: **current strategic decision record**

Snapshot date: **2026-08-14**

## 1. Purpose

Consolidate domain and digital-identity decisions without creating a second source of truth for brand architecture or page URLs.

Canonical brand identity lives in `brand/brand-foundation.md` and `README.md`.

Canonical URL architecture lives in `architecture/page-rendering-contract.md`.

## 2. Canonical naming hierarchy

Use this working hierarchy until final client approval:

- Canonical entity: **Melosa Clínica Brugal**.
- Preferred short commercial expression: **Clínica Brugal**.
- Historical/alternate expression: **Clínica Brugal Mejía López**.
- Legal name: **MELOSA CLINICA BRUGAL SRL**.
- Search alias only when context requires it: **Hospital Brugal**.

Do not treat these names as separate businesses.

Do not promote `Hospital Brugal` as the Spanish commercial brand solely for SEO.

## 3. Dominican-market language decision

Use `clínica` as the primary commercial category in Spanish.

The word `hospital` can appear descriptively when it matches real foreign-language search intent or contextual patient language.

It must not override the institution's commercial naming.

## 4. Social identity

Public social presence was observed using `melosaclinicabrugal` naming.

Preserve established handles unless the clinic approves a migration.

If new handles become available, reserve useful brand variants defensively when practical.

## 5. Ideal domain

The strongest brand domain remains:

`clinicabrugal.com`

Reasons:

- concise;
- memorable;
- matches the common short brand;
- uses the correct Spanish-market category;
- does not lock the institution to one city in the domain;
- can consolidate authority on one canonical property.

A prior RDAP check supplied during project research showed this domain as registered.

Revalidate ownership and availability before any acquisition decision.

## 6. Fallback domain strategy

If `clinicabrugal.com` cannot be recovered or acquired reasonably, the strongest previously identified fallback is:

`melosaclinicabrugal.com`

This aligns with the canonical entity and established social naming.

Other candidate domains can be defensive assets.

They should not host duplicate indexable sites.

## 8. Defensive domains

A defensive domain can protect brand variants or common user language.

Potential high-priority defensive variants:

- `hospitalbrugal.com`;
- `melosaclinicabrugal.com` when not canonical.

Potential secondary variants:

- `clinicabrugalmejialopez.com`;
- `clinicabrugalpuertoplata.com`;
- `clinicabrugalrd.com`.

Only one domain is canonical.

All defensive domains should redirect permanently after migration validation.

Do not serve canonical copies from multiple domains.

## 9. Existing corporate infrastructure

Research found public references to `melosa.com.do` and email addresses using that domain.

Do not replace or decommission existing corporate email/DNS infrastructure without a separate audit.

The public SEO domain and corporate email domain can remain different.

## 10. SEO principle

Do not make the domain carry the SEO strategy.

Organic authority must come from:

- real entity architecture;
- physician profiles;
- specialty pages;
- service and procedure pages;
- local entity consistency;
- Google Business Profile;
- reputation;
- useful medically reviewed content;
- structured data;
- internal linking;
- external references;
- reviewed English content where operationally justified.

## 11. Local SEO

Puerto Plata does not need to appear in the canonical domain to rank locally.

Reinforce locality through verified:

- address;
- phone;
- coordinates;
- service area/context;
- title and page copy;
- location pages;
- structured data;
- Google Business Profile;
- local references and links.

Operational NAP values must come from canonical Directus records.

Do not hardcode research values as permanent strategy facts.

## 12. English search intent

Keep reviewed English pages under the same canonical domain.

Use `/en/`.

Potential high-intent topics include:

- emergency care Puerto Plata;
- doctors in Puerto Plata;
- diagnostic imaging;
- laboratory services;
- private medical center;
- international-patient guidance.

The phrase `private hospital` can be used descriptively in English when truthful and useful.

It does not rename the institution.

## 13. Entity SEO

Google and other systems should be able to reconcile:

- Melosa Clínica Brugal;
- Clínica Brugal;
- Clínica Brugal Mejía López;
- legal identity;
- official domain;
- social profiles;
- verified address;
- verified phones;
- doctors;
- specialties;
- services;
- locations.

Use structured data only from visible, verified records.

## 14. Domain decision gate

Before production launch:

1. Revalidate `clinicabrugal.com` ownership and technical status.
2. Confirm the canonical domain with the clinic.
3. Audit `melosa.com.do` before changing DNS or email infrastructure.
4. Purchase defensive domains only when useful.
5. Configure one canonical origin.
6. Define redirects from all legacy or defensive domains.
7. Verify Search Console and Google Business Profile alignment.

## 15. Architecture dependency

Domain aliases must never create alternate content taxonomies.

All public content follows `architecture/page-rendering-contract.md` regardless of which defensive domain redirects to the canonical site.
