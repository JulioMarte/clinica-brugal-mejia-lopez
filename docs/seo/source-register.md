## Evidence handling rules

### Safe use

Use sources to:

- understand competitor information architecture;
- identify search-intent coverage;
- find potential entities requiring client verification;
- understand local market terminology;
- discover external references for subsequent validation.

### Do not treat as automatically verified

Do not copy or publish without client confirmation:

- physician schedules;
- physician rosters;
- insurance coverage;
- equipment claims;
- service availability;
- emergency hours;
- founding dates;
- awards;
- superlative claims;
- foreign-patient or language capabilities.

## Local business profile data

Google/local ratings and review counts were inspected during the competitive analysis.

Do not hard-code those values into project documentation as permanent facts. They are volatile metrics and should be captured only in dated SEO reports or monitoring data.

## Research limitations

The initial analysis is a strategic competitor review, not a complete Semrush/Ahrefs/Search Console dataset.

Before final production prioritization, collect or verify:

- indexed page counts;
- keyword rankings;
- search volumes;
- competitor backlinks;
- referring domains;
- Google Business Profile completeness;
- current physician rosters;
- current services;
- current insurance relationships;
- branded versus non-branded search demand;
- English-language local search opportunity.

## Citation rule for future documentation

Whenever a document makes a factual institutional claim, record the source and the verification status.

Recommended statuses:

- `verified`
- `needs_review`
- `historical`
- `deprecated`
- `competitive_observation`
- `time_sensitive`

This keeps SEO research separate from approved production facts.
