# SEO Keyword Strategy

Snapshot date: **2026-08-14**

## Principle

Keyword targeting must follow real patient intent and real clinic capabilities.

Do not create pages simply because a keyword has search volume. Build pages when Clínica Brugal can legitimately satisfy the intent behind the query.

## Priority keyword clusters

### 1. Brand intent

Examples:

- clinica brugal
- melosa clinica brugal
- clinica brugal puerto plata
- clinica brugal mejia lopez
- telefono clinica brugal
- clinica brugal citas
- clinica brugal medicos

Primary destinations:

- home page;
- contact page;
- appointment page;
- physician directory.

### 2. Local institutional intent

Examples:

- clinica puerto plata
- clinica en puerto plata
- clinica privada puerto plata
- centro medico puerto plata
- medicos puerto plata
- especialistas puerto plata

Primary destinations:

- home page;
- specialties directory;
- physician directory;
- institution/location pages.

Important terminology decision:

In Dominican Spanish, `clínica` is the preferred positioning term for a private medical institution. Do not make `hospital` the primary Spanish brand category solely for SEO purposes.

### 3. Emergency intent

Examples:

- emergencia puerto plata
- emergencia medica puerto plata
- clinica 24 horas puerto plata
- emergencia 24 horas puerto plata

Primary destination:

- emergency page.

Only target `24 horas` if current clinic operations verify that claim.

### 4. Specialty intent

Potential examples, subject to current service verification:

- cardiologo puerto plata
- pediatra puerto plata
- ginecologo puerto plata
- ortopeda puerto plata
- urologo puerto plata
- gastroenterologo puerto plata
- neurologo puerto plata
- otorrino puerto plata
- cirujano puerto plata

Primary destinations:

- specialty pages;
- relevant physician pages.

### 5. Diagnostic and procedure intent

Potential examples, subject to current capability verification:

- resonancia magnetica puerto plata
- tomografia puerto plata
- rayos x puerto plata
- sonografia puerto plata
- mamografia puerto plata
- laboratorio clinico puerto plata

Primary destinations:

- dedicated service or diagnostic pages.

These queries have direct vertical competitors. They should not resolve to one generic services page.

### 6. Physician-name intent

Pattern:

- doctor name puerto plata
- dra name puerto plata
- doctor name clinica brugal
- specialty + physician name

Primary destination:

- physician profile.

This cluster requires accurate, verified profiles and stable URLs.

### 7. English and visitor intent

Potential examples:

- doctor puerto plata
- medical clinic puerto plata
- emergency room puerto plata
- emergency doctor puerto plata
- medical care puerto plata
- English speaking doctor puerto plata
- diagnostic imaging puerto plata

Primary destinations:

- validated English pages under `/en/`.

Do not claim English-speaking service unless operationally verified.

## Search-intent hierarchy

### Highest transaction intent

- clinic brand + phone;
- doctor name + appointment;
- emergency searches;
- specialty + Puerto Plata;
- diagnostic procedure + Puerto Plata;
- laboratory + Puerto Plata.

These pages should be implemented before broad educational blogging.

### Medium transaction intent

- specialty explanations;
- doctor discovery;
- insurance questions;
- preparation for studies;
- visitor healthcare information.

### Informational intent

- symptoms;
- conditions;
- prevention;
- medical education.

Informational content should follow the core transactional architecture, not precede it.

## Page-to-keyword rule

Do not assign multiple pages to compete for the same primary intent without a clear reason.

Examples:

- `/especialidades/cardiologia/` owns the specialty-level cardiology intent.
- individual cardiologist profiles own physician-name intent.
- `/servicios/ecocardiograma/` owns service-specific ecocardiogram intent.

Internal linking connects them instead of creating duplicate local pages.

## Local modifiers

Puerto Plata is the principal local modifier.

Use it naturally in:

- title tags;
- H1 or supporting headings where useful;
- page copy;
- breadcrumbs where appropriate;
- address/location context;
- structured data.

Do not repeat `Puerto Plata` unnaturally throughout copy.

## Candidate title patterns

These are patterns, not final copy.

### Specialty

`Cardiología en Puerto Plata | Clínica Brugal`

### Physician

`Dr./Dra. [Nombre] — [Especialidad] | Clínica Brugal`

### Diagnostic service

`Resonancia Magnética en Puerto Plata | Clínica Brugal`

### Emergency

`Emergencias Médicas en Puerto Plata | Clínica Brugal`

Final titles must reflect verified service names and actual page content.

## Content quality requirements

Every high-intent medical landing page should provide materially useful information.

Avoid:

- keyword stuffing;
- duplicate city pages;
- automatically generated physician claims;
- fake FAQ content;
- unsupported superlatives;
- fabricated equipment specifications;
- copied competitor content;
- thin AI-generated medical pages;
- pages for services the clinic does not provide.

## Measurement plan

Track performance by cluster rather than one headline keyword.

Recommended groups:

1. Brand.
2. Clinic/local.
3. Emergency.
4. Specialty.
5. Physician.
6. Diagnostic imaging.
7. Laboratory.
8. English/visitor.

Measure:

- indexed URLs;
- impressions;
- clicks;
- non-brand organic traffic;
- calls;
- WhatsApp/contact actions;
- appointment starts;
- appointment completions when measurable;
- direction/map actions when measurable;
- organic landing page conversion rate.
