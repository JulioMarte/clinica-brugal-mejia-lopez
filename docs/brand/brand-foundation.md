# Brand Foundation — Melosa Clínica Brugal

## 1. Brand identity

### Primary brand

**Melosa Clínica Brugal**

### Short form

**Clínica Brugal**

### Existing alternate public form

**Clínica Brugal Mejía López**

Use this only where historical recognition or institutional context makes it useful.

### Legal name

**MELOSA CLINICA BRUGAL SRL**

Reserve the legal name for contracts, invoices, policies, fiscal documents, and other legal uses.

## 2. Category

Preferred Spanish descriptor:

**Clínica privada y centro médico especializado en Puerto Plata.**

Preferred English descriptor:

**Private medical center in Puerto Plata, Dominican Republic.**

Do not rename the institution “Hospital Brugal” for the Dominican market. The word “hospital” can be used descriptively in international SEO copy when it matches foreign search intent, but it is not the commercial name.

## 3. Brand essence

The brand rests on four concepts:

1. **Historia** — long-standing medical presence in Puerto Plata.
2. **Medicina** — specialized medical and diagnostic care.
3. **Familia** — generational continuity in the institution.
4. **Humanidad** — personal, respectful patient care.

## 4. Strategic territory

> **La clínica histórica de Puerto Plata que sigue avanzando con la medicina.**

The brand should not try to win through unsupported superlatives such as “largest,” “best,” or “most advanced.” Its strongest defensible territory is the combination of historical continuity, local recognition, medical specialization, and human care.

## 5. Positioning statement

**Melosa Clínica Brugal es una institución médica privada de Puerto Plata que combina décadas de tradición asistencial con medicina especializada, diagnóstico, hospitalización, emergencia y atención humana personalizada.**

## 6. Purpose

**Cuidar la salud de las personas mediante medicina especializada y atención humana.**

## 7. Brand promise

**Atención especializada sin perder el trato humano.**

## 8. Existing institutional language

### Philosophy

**Medicina especializada con trato humano personalizado.**

This is already part of the institution’s public communication and should be preserved.

### Tagline

**Cuidarte es amarte.**

Use as the emotional tagline.

### Recommended message hierarchy

- **Brand:** Melosa Clínica Brugal
- **Tagline:** Cuidarte es amarte.
- **Philosophy:** Medicina especializada con trato humano personalizado.

## 9. Campaign-level brand line

**Generaciones de medicina. Décadas de historia. Una misma vocación: cuidar.**

Use for brand campaigns, history content, presentations, video, and institutional storytelling. Do not treat it as a replacement for the existing tagline without explicit client approval.

## 10. Brand pillars

### Trust

Communicate stability, clarity, medical competence, and reliability through evidence.

### History

Present institutional history as a source of continuity and local credibility.

### Human care

Show people caring for people. Avoid cold, purely technological messaging.

### Specialization

Explain actual medical disciplines, services, qualifications, equipment, and clinical capabilities.

### Availability

Make emergency access, contact, appointments, directions, and current hours easy to find.

### Community

Puerto Plata is part of the identity, not just an address.

### Evolution

Show modernization without erasing the historical character of the clinic.

## 11. Brand personality

The brand should feel:

- trustworthy;
- human;
- experienced;
- professional;
- stable;
- organized;
- clean;
- locally rooted;
- modern without appearing experimental.

The brand should not feel:

- cold;
- futuristic;
- elitist;
- ostentatious;
- like a startup;
- like a cosmetic spa;
- like an insurance company;
- like a government portal.

## 12. Archetypes

### Primary: Caregiver — approximately 65%

Represents empathy, protection, service, guidance, and care.

### Secondary: Sage — approximately 35%

Represents medicine, knowledge, experience, science, and specialization.

The brand should lead emotionally with care and support its claims with expertise.

## 13. Voice

The voice is:

**clear + competent + human.**

### Principles

- Explain before impressing.
- Use direct patient language.
- Use medical terminology only when useful.
- Explain technical terms when necessary.
- Prefer evidence to adjectives.
- Avoid hype.
- Avoid fear-based marketing.
- Avoid medical guarantees.

### Correct example

> Nuestro equipo de Cardiología evalúa y trata distintas enfermedades cardiovasculares mediante consulta especializada y estudios diagnósticos.

### Avoid

> Contamos con los mejores cardiólogos y la tecnología más avanzada del país.

The second statement contains unsupported absolute claims and generic advertising language.

## 14. Tone by context

### Emergency

Direct, concise, operational.

### Prevention and education

Clear, educational, calm.

### Social media

Human and accessible.

### Institutional communication

Professional and restrained.

### Cardiovascular and other specialized care

Precise, evidence-based, and easy to understand.

### International patients

Practical, reassuring, and explicit about logistics.

### Pediatrics

Warm, family-centered, and safe.

## 16. Language to limit

Avoid or substantiate before use:

- líder;
- mejor;
- número uno;
- único;
- más moderno;
- más grande;
- revolucionario;
- incomparable;
- excelencia absoluta.

## 17. CTA vocabulary

Preferred:

- Agendar cita
- Buscar médico
- Ver especialidad
- Llamar ahora
- Conocer el servicio
- Cómo llegar
- Consultar seguro
- Escribir por WhatsApp

Avoid vague lifestyle-marketing CTA language such as “Descubre,” “Transforma tu salud,” or “Vive una nueva experiencia.”

## 18. Brand architecture

Use a **branded house** model.

### Master brand

**Melosa Clínica Brugal**

### Service families

- Emergencia
- Emergencia Pediátrica
- Centro Cardiovascular
- Imágenes y Diagnóstico
- Laboratorio
- Hospitalización
- UCI
- Cirugía
- Consultas Especializadas
- Pacientes Internacionales

Specialized units can retain recognized subnames, but the master brand should remain visible. Example:

**Centro Cardiovascular Dr. Mejía — Melosa Clínica Brugal**

## 19. Claims currently safe for working drafts

Subject to current operational confirmation before launch:

- Medicina especializada con trato humano personalizado.
- Emergencia disponible las 24 horas.
- Atención para pacientes nacionales e internacionales.
- Múltiples especialidades médicas y quirúrgicas.
- Servicios de diagnóstico por imágenes.
- Unidad de Cuidados Intensivos.
- Centro Cardiovascular.
- Resonancia Magnética.
- Radiología Intervencionista.

## 20. Claims blocked until validation

Do not publish as final claims:

- Fundada en 1936.
- Fundada en 1973.
- Primera clínica de la región.
- La clínica privada más grande de la costa norte.
- La clínica más moderna.
- La mayor institución sanitaria privada del país.

## 21. Historical narrative framework

The recommended narrative sequence is:

**Panchito Brugal → Clínica Brugal → Mejía-López → nuevas generaciones → medicina contemporánea.**

This preserves historical recognition while explaining the current institution as an evolution rather than a disconnected new brand.

## 22. North star

Every brand or design decision should pass this test:

> **¿Esto parece una institución médica de Puerto Plata con décadas de historia y atención humana?**

If the output instead resembles a SaaS company, luxury clinic, insurance company, or generic corporate hospital, it is outside the intended brand territory.
