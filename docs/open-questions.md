# Open Questions and Client Validation Checklist

## Purpose

This file contains information that must be confirmed directly with Melosa Clínica Brugal before final publication. Items here are intentionally separated from approved brand direction so provisional research does not accidentally become permanent website copy.

## 1. Historical identity

- What is the institution’s official documented founding date?
- What does the year **1936** refer to?
- What does the year **1973** refer to, and should it be considered an error in the current site?
- How should the 1944 Simpson/Loinaz/Brugal association be described officially?
- Is **1952** the accepted date for Dr. Francisco Brugal’s own clinic?
- What was the exact historical legal/commercial name in each period?
- Which historical photographs and documents can the clinic authorize for publication?
- Does the clinic have original photographs of Dr. Francisco “Panchito” Brugal?
- Are there historical logos or seals?

## 2. 2001 transition

- Confirm the exact acquisition date.
- Confirm the correct names and titles of the Mejía-López owners involved.
- Confirm how the name **Melosa Clínica Brugal** originated.
- Confirm which family members belong to the first and second generations of physicians referenced publicly.
- Identify major expansions or renovations since 2001.

Client should approve one canonical public naming hierarchy:
