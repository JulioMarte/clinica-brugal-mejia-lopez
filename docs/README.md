# Clínica Brugal Mejía López — Project Documentation

This folder is the source of truth for the branding, institutional research, content strategy, SEO strategy, and web implementation of Melosa Clínica Brugal.

## Purpose

The project does not start from a blank brand. Melosa Clínica Brugal already has a recognized name, a blue-and-white visual identity, a historical seal, a long relationship with Puerto Plata, an existing philosophy, and a broad clinical offering. The work documented here organizes those assets, separates verified facts from provisional claims, and translates the existing identity into a consistent digital system.

The website must also function as a structured, indexable representation of the real institution. It must connect the clinic with its physicians, specialties, services, procedures, local presence, and patient actions.

## Core direction

The central brand territory is:

> **A historic medical institution of Puerto Plata that continues to evolve with medicine.**

The digital identity must communicate two ideas at the same time:

> **We have cared for generations.**

and

> **We continue to advance with medicine.**

## SEO strategic direction

The website should model this relationship explicitly:

> **Clínica Brugal → Puerto Plata → specialties → physicians → services → procedures → patient intent → appointment/contact.**

The current principal SEO benchmark is **Centro Médico Dr. Bournigal** because it exposes a larger indexable surface across services, specialties, physicians, and appointments.

The opportunity for Clínica Brugal is broader than ranking for one phrase such as `clínica Puerto Plata`. The project should build useful pages around the real medical entities and high-intent patient needs that the clinic can actually satisfy.

Important market decision:

- In Dominican Spanish, `clínica` is the preferred positioning term for a private medical institution.
- Do not reposition the Spanish brand primarily around `hospital` solely for keyword targeting.

Puerto Plata also creates a meaningful English-language opportunity because of tourism and foreign residents. English pages must reflect verified operational capabilities and should not be published as unsupported machine-translated claims.

## Status model

Every important institutional fact should eventually be classified as one of:

- `verified` — safe to publish based on approved or reliable current evidence.
- `needs_review` — plausible or publicly stated, but requires direct institutional confirmation.
- `historical` — supported as historical context but not necessarily current operational information.
- `deprecated` — old, conflicting, superseded, or explicitly rejected information.
- `competitive_observation` — a dated observation about another entity or search result.
- `time_sensitive` — information that requires periodic revalidation.

This distinction is especially important for doctors, schedules, insurance coverage, service availability, historical dates, ratings, opening hours, equipment claims, and superlative marketing claims.

## Current critical caution

The existing public website contains conflicting foundation dates, including references to 1936 and 1973. Independent historical material better supports a clinical association in the 1940s and the creation of Dr. Francisco “Panchito” Brugal's own clinic in 1952. Do not publish a definitive founding year until documentary evidence from the institution resolves the conflict.

## Working principle

The website must first help a patient complete a task:

1. Find a doctor.
2. Find a specialty or service.
3. Book an appointment.
4. Contact emergency care.
5. Find the clinic and get directions.

History, brand storytelling, institutional news, and educational content support trust, but they must not block these primary tasks.

## SEO implementation principle

Do not build a thin brochure website with one generic services page.

Give real high-value entities their own structured content when justified:

- physician;
- specialty;
- diagnostic service;
- procedure;
- emergency care;
- laboratory;
- patient information;
- insurance information;
- English visitor information.

Do not create doorway pages, keyword-stuffed city pages, fake FAQs, fabricated medical claims, or pages for services that the clinic does not provide.
